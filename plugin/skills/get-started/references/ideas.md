# Ideas by kind of organizer

Find the row that matches what the person runs, then show three or four of its ideas. Each idea
names the page type, the template it starts from, and what to tell the person it does for them.

**Rules for using this file:**

- The template slugs come from PayIt2's template catalog as of 2026-11-21. The live list can
  differ. When PayIt2 tools are available, call `list_templates` and use a slug only if it came
  back. When they are not, do not name slugs at all: describe the idea in words.
- The links are sections of payit2.com that were checked on 2026-11-21. Use only links from this
  file. Do not build other payit2.com addresses.
- Rewrite each idea in the person's own words for their group. The lines below are starting
  points, not scripts.

## School group, PTA or boosters

Browse: https://www.payit2.com/use-cases/schools-clubs/

| Idea | Page type | Template | What it does for them |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fall festival or trunk-or-treat tickets | Event | `fall-festival`, `trunk-or-treat` | Families buy ahead, adults and kids on one order, and you know the headcount before the day |
| School fundraiser | Fundraiser | `school-fundraiser` | A goal, a progress bar and giving levels on a page you can send to every family |
| Spirit wear orders | Group payment | `spirit-wear` | Parents pick sizes and pay in one place, and you get one list for the printer |
| Field trip or student fees | Group payment | `field-trip`, `student-fees` | Each family pays their share by the deadline, and you see who has not paid |
| School dance tickets | Event | `school-dance` | Tickets sold ahead, with a list to check at the door |

## Helping a person or family in need

Browse: https://www.payit2.com/use-cases/medical-funds/ and https://www.payit2.com/use-cases/emergency-relief/

| Idea | Page type | Template | What it does for them |
|---|---|---|---|

For this kind of organizer, an example page uses a made-up cause, never the real person. See Step 4 of the skill.
